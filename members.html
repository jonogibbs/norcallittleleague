For Members

Who Can Be a Member

Regular Member
Any person actively serving as a District Administrator in Northern California. Regular Members vote and may serve on the Board.
Emeritus Member
A former District Administrator elected by vote of the Members.
Advisory Member
A person approved by the Members to advise on a specific project or area of expertise.

Meetings and Fiscal Year

The annual meeting of Members is held on the third Saturday in October, unless the Board approves another day in October. Regular meetings are held at least once a year, in the fall. Members receive at least 14 days’ notice of each meeting.

The fiscal year runs October 1 through September 30.

Questions

Members can contact the Secretary through norcallittleleague@gmail.com.
