About the Association

Who We Are

The Northern California Little League District Administrators Association, Inc. is a California nonprofit public benefit corporation, incorporated on July 6, 2023. Our members are the Little League District Administrators serving communities across Northern California.

We are a recognized member of Little League Baseball, Incorporated, and our federal tax exemption is covered under Little League’s group exemption under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code. That exemption applies only to chartered Little League programs.

What We Do

  • Facilitate cooperation among Members for the benefit of all Little League participants in Northern California.
  • Seek consensus among Little League districts in Northern California on tournament hosting standards and on the conduct of the Northern California Championship level of the Little League International Tournament.
  • Review changes in Little League rules to promote a common interpretation and application of those rules in Northern California.
  • Act for the benefit of all participants in the Little League Baseball, Softball, and Challenger Programs.

Governance

The Association is managed by a volunteer Board of Directors elected by its Members at the annual meeting. The Board consists of a President, Vice President, Secretary, Treasurer, Baseball Director, and Softball Director. Directors must be District Administrators.
