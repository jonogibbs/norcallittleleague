Tournaments

Northern California Championship

The Association works with Little League districts and sections to set common standards for hosting the Northern California Championship level of the Little League International Tournament, and to promote consistent application of Little League rules across the region.

Divisions

Our work covers the Little League Baseball, Softball, and Challenger programs. Our Baseball Director and Softball Director act as liaisons between the Association and the districts and sections for their respective divisions.

Hosting

Each section confirms a tournament host rotation for the age divisions of the Little League International Tournament. Tournament sites are established at our regular Member meeting each fall. Hosts must be in good standing with the Association.

Questions about hosting or tournament standards? Email us.
